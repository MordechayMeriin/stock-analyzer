reports.

fiscal years: for each one, its title, the year pair, its status (changed, new or dropped), its
text similarity, and the full text of both versions (or of the one version that exists).

For each section, say in one or two sentences what actually changed in substance: a new or ended
activity, customer, contract, segment, acquisition, regulation, risk, lawsuit, strategy, structure
or management. Ignore wording changes, dates rolled forward and figures updated without a change
in substance; when a section only changed that way, say so with kind "routine". Cite the paragraph
ids of the old and the new version.
